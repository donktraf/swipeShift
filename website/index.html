How it works

Who SwipeShift is for

For workers

  • Browse shifts in a list, on a map, or swipe through them.
  • Apply to one or several shifts of a job.
  • Chat with the employer once you are accepted.
  • Keep your calendar and earnings overview in one place.

For employers

  • Post a job with one or more shifts in minutes.
  • Review applicants, accept them, or use Instant Match.
  • Track staffing, attendance, and payments per job.
  • Rate workers after the shift.

Why SwipeShift

Fast

Find work starting today or tomorrow, or fill a shift in hours instead of days.

Flexible

Choose single shifts that fit your life. No long-term commitment required.

Fair and transparent

Workers receive the full advertised hourly rate. Employers pay a clear platform fee on top. Two-way ratings keep everyone accountable.
