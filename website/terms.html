4. Fees and payment

  • Workers receive the full hourly rate stated in the job. We do not deduct a fee from worker pay.
  • Employers pay a platform fee, currently 15% of the workers’ wages, in addition to the wages. The rate that applies to a job is fixed when the job is published.
  • At present, wages are paid by the employer to the worker outside the App, and employers mark workers as paid in the App. SwipeShift does not currently process or guarantee payments. We may introduce in-app payments later and will update these terms.
  • Employers must pay workers promptly and in full for hours worked.

5. Cancellations and no-shows

  • Workers may cancel a booked shift. A cancellation less than 24 hours before the start of that shift is a late cancellation, assessed per shift.
  • Employers record attendance after a shift as attended, late, or no-show. If an employer records nothing, the shift is recorded as attended after 48 hours.
  • No-shows and late cancellations are part of a worker’s reliability record, which employers can see. Repeated no-shows or late cancellations may lead to warnings or suspension.
  • A worker can dispute a recorded attendance once, within 14 days. A SwipeShift moderator decides the dispute.
  • Employers may cancel jobs and shifts. Employers who cancel repeatedly or at short notice may also face warnings or suspension. [ANY COMPENSATION RULES FOR SHORT-NOTICE EMPLOYER CANCELLATIONS — TO BE DECIDED]
