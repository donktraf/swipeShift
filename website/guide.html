Help & Guide

Answers to common questions about using SwipeShift. Can’t find what you need? Email support@swipeshift.app.

Finding and booking shifts

How do I apply for a job?

Open a job and tap Apply. If the job has one shift, you confirm that shift. If it has several shifts, you first choose the shift or shifts you can work and then confirm. You can only apply to shifts that do not overlap with shifts you have already booked.

The employer reviews your profile and replies in the chat. You are not committed to anything until the employer accepts your application.

How does Swipe work?

In the Swipe tab, jobs appear as cards. Swipe right to apply and swipe left to skip. For jobs with several shifts, swiping right opens the shift selection first. You can also bookmark a job to save it for later.

What is Instant Match?

Some employers turn on Instant Match for a job. When you apply to such a job, your application is accepted automatically and the chat with the employer opens right away.

Cancelling a shift

How do I cancel a shift I booked?

Open the job in your applications or calendar and choose to cancel. If you booked several shifts, you can cancel individual shifts. If you cancel your last booked shift, the whole application is withdrawn.

What is a late cancellation?

A cancellation counts as late when you cancel less than 24 hours before the start of that particular shift. The 24-hour rule is applied per shift, so cancelling one shift late does not make an earlier cancellation of another shift late. Late cancellations are part of your reliability record (see below).

Reliability and no-shows

What is the reliability record?

After a shift, the employer records whether you attended, were late, or did not show up. If the employer does not record anything, the shift is marked as attended automatically after 48 hours. Your attendance, no-shows, and late cancellations from the last 12 months are summarized as a reliability record that employers can see when they review your profile.

What if a no-show or late mark is wrong?

You can submit one dispute per recorded shift. Open the shift, explain what happened, and send it. You must do this within 14 days of the attendance being recorded. A SwipeShift moderator reviews your dispute and can change the record.

Pay and fees

How much do workers get paid?

Workers receive the full hourly rate shown on the job. SwipeShift never deducts a fee from the worker’s pay.

What does the employer pay?

Employers pay the workers’ wages plus a platform fee of 15% on top of the wages. The fee that applies to a job is fixed when the job is published.

How does payment work today?

At the moment, employers pay workers outside the app. Employers mark workers as paid inside SwipeShift after they have paid them, and workers see the status in their financial overview. Payments are not processed by SwipeShift yet.

What are payout details?

Workers can save US bank details (account holder name, routing number, account type, and account number) in Account Settings. The account number is stored encrypted, and only the last four digits are shown back to you. Changing payout details requires you to confirm your identity again.

Safety

How do I report or block someone?

In a chat, use the report option to report the other person and choose a reason. You can also block them. When you block someone, neither of you can send messages in the chat anymore, and old messages stay visible. Our team reviews reports.

What happens to people who break the rules?

SwipeShift has zero tolerance for offensive content and abuse. Moderators can warn, temporarily suspend, or permanently ban accounts. See the community rules in our Terms of Service.

Your account

How do I pause or delete my account?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Data. Pausing hides your activity: workers cannot apply, and an employer’s jobs are hidden. Deleting your account removes your profile and data as described in our Privacy Policy. Employers: you cannot delete your account while shifts that have already ended are still unpaid; pay your workers first (Jobs → Payments). Once everything is paid, a job that is already running stays on record as completed, only its upcoming shifts are cancelled, and the affected workers are notified. If you cannot sign in, email support@swipeshift.app from the address on your account and we will help.

How do I turn on two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Go to Settings → Account & Security. You can use a code sent to your email or an authenticator app. With an authenticator app you also get one-time backup codes. In the same place you can see your active sessions and sign out other devices.
