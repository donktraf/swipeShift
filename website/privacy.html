1. Who we are

SwipeShift (the “App”) is a marketplace that connects workers with employers for short-term shifts. This policy explains what personal information [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (“SwipeShift”, “we”, “us”), [ADDRESS], collects through the App and how we use it. You must be at least 18 years old to use SwipeShift.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the App, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored only as a hash), role (worker or employer), date of birth (workers), phone number, and profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them, and for Apple a stable Apple account identifier.
  • Worker profile: bio, preferred job categories, work experience by category, skills, availability (days, times of day, shift types, same-day availability), willingness to relocate, maximum travel distance, mode of transport, pay range, and whether you have work authorization.
  • Employer profile: company name, size, industry, logo, description, website or social links, company phone and email, business areas, locations and address, team members, and your job title and role.
  • Location: the city or address you enter in your profile, and the locations of jobs employers post. We convert these into map coordinates to show distances and the Radar map. The App does not use your device’s GPS location.
  • Jobs and applications: jobs you post, jobs you apply to, skip, or save, the shifts you book or cancel (including cancellation reasons), companies you save, and applicants employers save.
  • Messages: text messages you exchange in chats with the other party of an accepted application, and system messages about the job.
  • Ratings and reviews: ratings and comments employers give workers (communication, teamwork, reliability, working under pressure, would rehire) and that workers give employers; drafts of employer reviews.
  • Reliability data: attendance recorded for each shift (attended, late, no-show), late cancellations, and any dispute you submit. Employers can see a summary of this for workers who apply to them.
  • Safety reports: reports you file about other users (reason and optional details), blocks you set, and the moderation decisions we take (warnings, suspensions, bans).
  • Payout and payment data: US bank account details (account holder name, routing number, account type, and account number, which is stored encrypted, plus its last four digits), and the paid/unpaid status of shifts. SwipeShift does not currently move money.
  • Settings and consents: notification preferences, quiet hours, payout preferences, and the time you accepted our terms and rating-system terms.
  • Communications with us: emails you send to support.

Information collected automatically

  • Sessions and devices: for each signed-in device we store device name and model, operating system version, the IP address used at sign-in, an approximate city, and timestamps of activity. The approximate city is taken from the city in your profile and sent by the App; we do not look up your location from your IP address. IP addresses are also used to limit repeated failed two-factor attempts and to prevent abuse.
  • Time zone: your device time zone, used to respect your quiet hours for push notifications.
  • Push notification token: a token issued by Apple so we can send push notifications to your device, if you allow them.
  • Last-active time and read status: when you were last active and whether chat messages and notifications were read, used for chat presence (such as typing and read indicators) and unread badges.
  • Two-factor authentication: if you enable it, a one-time email code is sent when you sign in, or we store your authenticator secret (encrypted) and hashed backup codes.
  • Technical logs: our servers keep standard operational logs. [CONFIRM LOG CONTENT AND RETENTION]

3. How we use data and who sees it

  • To provide the service: create and secure your account, match workers with jobs, show distances, process applications and bookings, enable chat, track attendance, ratings, and payment status.
  • Communication: send verification and password-reset emails, shift reminders (24 hours and 2 hours before a booked shift), alerts about applications, cancellations, payments, and unread messages, by push notification and email according to your settings. Verification and password-reset emails are always sent.
  • Safety and fairness: review reports, enforce our community rules, detect abuse, and prevent banned people from returning (see Retention).
  • Security: sign-in protection, two-factor authentication, session management.

What other users can see

  • Employers see a worker’s profile when the worker applies: name, photo, rating, experience, skills, location, availability, reliability summary, and work authorization status.
  • Workers see the employer’s company profile, job postings, ratings, and reviews. Other parties in a chat see your name, photo, and messages.
  • Other people do not see your email address, date of birth, or payout details.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information to service providers (below), when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in a merger or sale of the business.

4. Service providers

  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: if you choose them, to authenticate you.
  • Apple Push Notification service: to deliver push notifications (receives your device token and the notification text).
  • Apple MapKit: the App uses Apple Maps for the Radar map and address suggestions on your device.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: our servers send the location text from profiles and job posts (such as a city or address) to Nominatim to obtain coordinates. No account identifier is sent.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account exists. Sessions expire after 7 days of sign-in validity.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the App under Settings → Privacy & Data, or by emailing support@swipeshift.app from the email address of the account. You can email us even if your account is suspended or banned. Deletion removes your profile, applications, saved items, sessions, push tokens, and the chats and reviews connected to your applications.

Requires legal/engineering confirmation: whether uploaded profile photos and company logos on our storage, job postings of deleted employers, backups, and logs are also removed, and how quickly.

What we keep after deletion

  • Safety reports and moderation records about or by you are kept so we can protect other users, even after deletion. [CONFIRM RETENTION PERIOD]
  • Abuse-prevention record: if your account had reports or moderation actions, or was suspended or banned, we keep a minimal record when you delete it: a keyed hash (HMAC) of your email address, a hash of your Apple account identifier if you used Apple sign-in, your role, a short display name, and the dates of deletion, suspension, or ban. This lets us stop a banned person from registering again. Without a ban, this record is deleted 90 days after deletion. With a ban, it is kept until the ban is lifted (and then for 90 days).
  • Information we must keep to meet legal obligations.

6. Security

We protect data with measures such as hashed passwords, encrypted bank account numbers and authenticator secrets, hashed backup codes, session revocation, and re-authentication before payout details can be changed. No system is perfectly secure.

8. Your choices

  • Push and email notifications per category, and quiet hours, in Settings.
  • Pause your account in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Edit or delete profile information in the App; delete your account at any time.

9. Age requirement

SwipeShift is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.
